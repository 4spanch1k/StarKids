# Boom Bala image assets

`home_hero_generated.png` is the Home primary hero. It was generated for the
production UI with this prompt:

> Premium lifestyle advertising photography inside a modern high-end indoor
> family entertainment center, joyful child and/or family enjoying the play
> zone naturally, sophisticated family brand aesthetic, warm natural cinematic
> lighting, realistic skin and proportions, editorial commercial photography,
> clean composition, elegant depth, playful but premium atmosphere, enough calm
> negative space on the left for mobile UI text overlay, main subject
> center-right, no text, no logo, no watermark.

Existing `birthday_hero.jpg` and `promo_hero.jpg` remain repository assets and
are used by their respective real API-backed surfaces. API-provided media
takes precedence over these local fallbacks.
